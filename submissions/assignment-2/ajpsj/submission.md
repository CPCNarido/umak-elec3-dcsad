### B4. Predict a change

Can you still open the web page from your laptop? Why?

No. Deleting the 0.0.0.0/0 route breaks the path between the public subnet and the Internet Gateway. Without this route, HTTP responses generated by the web server can'tt leave the VPC to reach your laptop across the internet.

Can the instance still reach another instance in the VPC? Why?

Yes. Intra-VPC communications rely on the local route, which automatically directs traffic between subnets inside the same VPC. Removing the 0.0.0.0/0 internet route still leaves the local route active.
